    How the maps are made. Hypixel sends the whole map while everyone waits before the start. The Atlas keeps the map from that moment, takes away the waiting lobby above the middle, and leaves out the island toppers added at the start and everything players do later. Several games of one map are stitched together, and the newest game wins if Hypixel changed the map.

    The rotation list is kept by players (JaayMC's Bed Wars map list) and updated each Monday.
